# Task card — reliability contract under stochastic power

- **Task ID:** `factory.power.contract.v1`
- **Version:** 0.1.0
- **Status:** P0 benchmark candidate; not part of frozen `factory-v0.1`
- **World:** factory-0.1.0
- **Owner:** FABLINE
- **Date:** 2026-11-21

## Phenomenon

A factory commits to a delivery target before an episode runs. Meeting the
target earns a bonus; missing it incurs a fixed penalty. Under stochastic
generator capacity and load, the target that maximizes expected payoff can
differ from the target that maximizes tail payoff.

This is a one-shot commitment decision layered on the power-allocation control
task. The target does not change the world; it changes only the payoff.

## Contract

- **Observation:** none for the target choice; the stochastic schedule is
  hidden.
- **Action:** choose a target from `80, 85, …, 150`.
- **Operating policy:** scripted alternator (fixed, not learned).
- **Underlying episode:** gear line, one generator, capacity 5, radius 8,
  500 ticks, decision interval 10.
- **Stochastic power:** load jitter 60, capacity jitter 40, period 20.
- **Payoff:** production score `s`, plus `T - 100` when `s >= T`, minus 40 when
  `s < T`.
- **Selection:** offline over logged episode scores; not online bandit learning.

## Data (0031 formal)

- Train blocks: even 600–998; test blocks: odd 601–999.
- 10 episodes per block → 2,000 unique train and 2,000 unique test episodes.
- Episode seeds expand as `block * 10 + episode`; train and test are disjoint.
- No dataset artifact; logs are regenerated by the runner.
- Public harness: `src/fabline/harness/reliability_contract.py`; reproduce
  with `python -m fabline reliability-contract --out results/reliability-contract`.
  The public run selects the same 120/110 targets and measures a +17.62 CVaR
  gain [13.90, 21.68], consistent with the 0031 formal result (+17.05
  [13.25, 21.15]).

## Metrics

- mean payoff;
- CVaR₂₀ payoff (mean of the worst 20%);
- worst payoff;
- target hit rate;
- block-bootstrap 95% CI for the CVaR gain.

## Known pitfalls

- **Sampling defect corrected.** The original runner expanded blocks as
  `seed + episode`, so adjacent blocks overlapped. Always expand as
  `block * episodes_per_seed + episode` and check uniqueness.
- **Gate history.** The original +15 gate came from the contaminated pilot; the
  clean, replicated effect is +13 to +17 CVaR for about 5 mean points. The +10
  confirmation gate was set from 0030’s lower CI bound before 0031.
- **Offline selection.** Counterfactual payoffs are computed from logged
  scores; this is not an online learning result.
- **Fixed operating policy.** The scripted alternator is not adapted to the
  contract.
- **One failure-dense regime and one miss penalty.** Both were frozen before
  the formal runs.
- **Commitment, not sequential control.** The target is chosen once per
  episode.
- **Tail fraction sensitivity.** The effect varies with alpha; gates use 0.2.
- **Block bootstrap assumes exchangeable blocks.** It does not model long-range
  seed drift.
- **No context.** The target choice does not condition on early observations.
- Not part of frozen `factory-v0.1`.

## Negative controls

- Fixed targets above 145 should have low hit rates and poor tail payoff.
- The mean-optimal target should not also dominate CVaR.
- The risk-safe target should not increase mean payoff.
- A correct sampler must report zero train/test episode-seed overlap.
