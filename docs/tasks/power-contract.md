# Task card — reliability contract under stochastic power

- **Task ID:** `factory.power.contract.v1`
- **Version:** 0.1.0
- **Status:** debug/P0 task; not part of `factory-v0.1`
- **World:** factory-0.1.0
- **Owner:** FABLINE
- **Date:** 2026-11-21

## Phenomenon

A factory commits to a delivery target before an episode runs. Meeting the
target earns a bonus; missing it incurs a fixed penalty. Under stochastic
generator capacity and load, the target that maximizes expected payoff can
differ from the target that maximizes tail payoff.

This is a one-shot commitment decision layered on the power-allocation control
task. The target does not change the world; it changes only the payoff.

## Contract

- **Observation:** none for the target choice; the stochastic schedule is
  hidden.
- **Action:** choose a target from `120, 125, 130, 135, 140, 145, 150, 155`.
- **Operating policy:** scripted alternator (fixed, not learned).
- **Underlying episode:** gear line, one generator, capacity 5, radius 8,
  500 ticks, decision interval 10.
- **Stochastic power:** load jitter 30, capacity jitter 20, period 20.
- **Payoff:** production score `s`, plus `T - 120` when `s >= T`, minus 40 when
  `s < T`.
- **Selection:** offline over logged episode scores; not online bandit learning.

## Data

- Train: seeds 40–79, 10 episodes each = 400 scores.
- Test: seeds 80–99, 10 episodes each = 200 scores.
- No dataset artifact; logs are regenerated by the runner.

## Metrics

- mean payoff;
- CVaR₂₀ payoff (mean of the worst 20%);
- worst payoff;
- target hit rate.

## Baselines

| Baseline | Notes |
|---|---|
| fixed target | every tier, evaluated on test |
| mean-optimal | target with best train mean |
| CVaR-optimal | target with best train CVaR₂₀ |

## Results

| policy | test mean | test CVaR₂₀ |
|---|---:|---:|
| mean-optimal (130) | 138.90 | 104.00 |
| CVaR-optimal (120) | 131.40 | 106.50 |

The mean/CVaR targets separate (130 vs 120), but the pre-registered 20% tail
gain is only +2.5, below the +15 gate. The effect is concentrated in the worst
5–10% (exploratory +20.0 / +15.0). See
[`experiments/0028-risk-contract`](../experiments/0028-risk-contract/summary.md).

## Known pitfalls

- **Seed-clustered tail.** Seeds 95–99 each contain one zero-score episode;
  seeds 80–94 are benign. Tail estimates depend on the test block.
- **Offline selection.** Counterfactual payoffs are computed from logged
  scores; this is not an online learning result.
- **Fixed operating policy.** The scripted alternator is not adapted to the
  contract.
- **One jitter regime and one miss penalty.** Both were frozen from a design
  pilot.
- **Commitment, not sequential control.** The target is chosen once per
  episode.
- **Tail fraction sensitivity.** CVaR₂₀ dilutes a clustered extreme tail; the
  result is not robust to alpha.
- Not part of `factory-v0.1`.

## Negative controls

- Fixed targets above 145 should have low hit rates and poor tail payoff.
- The mean-optimal target should not also dominate CVaR.
- The risk-safe target should not increase mean payoff.
