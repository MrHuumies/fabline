# ADR 0044 — 0.8.0 scope: spatial production grammar

- **Status:** proposed; owner approval pending
- **Date:** 2026-11-28
- **Related:** RFC 0033, RFC 0032, ADR 0043,
  [`roadmap-0.5.0-0.8.0.md`](../roadmap-0.5.0-0.8.0.md),
  [`coverage-map.md`](../coverage-map.md),
  [`factory-v0.6`](../../benchmarks/factory-v0.6/card.md)

## Context

0.7.0 froze `factory-v0.6` as a sequential-crew challenge; the task is valid
but the learned baselines fail. Phase 2 communication (0096) is negative:
local no-report 28.4/0.92 vs local report 17.2/0.72. The communication/report
line is retired on the current layout, and the multi-agent/uncertain-repair
line has no further cheap positive pilot.

The roadmap's 0.8.0 flagship is the **spatial production grammar**: larger and
randomized layouts, obstacles, splitters/mergers, routing, and scale transfer.
This is the largest remaining coverage gap (spatial/OOD/relational/routing) and
uses existing production semantics.

## Decision

1. 0.8.0 is the **spatial production grammar**, one flagship mechanic.
2. The first slice is an opt-in layout grammar + **layout-shift** task:
   generator tests, disjoint layout splits, throughput/bottleneck baselines,
   and an in-distribution vs OOD comparison.
3. The second slice is **route control** with splitters/mergers and a new
   action contract; it is gated on the generator and layout-shift task.
4. Scale transfer is the third slice; line balancing/inventory/quality are
   optional closers only if they add a new decision.
5. Stochastic faults/POMDP are **not** in 0.8.0. They remain a 0.9.0 or
   conditional closer; do not combine them with the spatial grammar.
6. The grammar is opt-in and procedural. The default world hash and frozen
   v0.1–v0.6 assets must remain unchanged.
7. Freeze `factory-v0.7` only after the generator and layout-shift task pass;
   a working generator without a separating task is a diagnostic.
8. Generative layout/recipe remains conditional on a grammar audit; do not
   train a model on the current small grammar.

## First slice

- `SpatialGrammarConfig` with width/height, obstacle density, line count,
  offsets/orientations, and optional splitters/mergers.
- Deterministic seeded generation, canonical state, replay, and item
  conservation.
- Layout-seed splits: train/val/test/shift; no episode-level leakage.
- `layout-shift` throughput/bottleneck task: train on base layouts, test on
  held-out larger/random layouts.
- Baselines: mean/persistence, linear/GBDT, MLP, CNN, MPNN, Transformer.
- Gates: skill over mean, OOD gap and model ordering, deterministic generation,
  no leakage, no default-hash change.

## Alternatives

- **Stochastic faults/POMDP first.** Deferred from 0.7.0; it is a second
  mechanic and the fault/calibration surface is already broad. Keep it for
  0.9.0 or a conditional closer.
- **Inventory/quality first.** Reuses the production surface but adds a
  resource/quality axis before the spatial grammar is tested; defer.
- **Generative layout first.** Conditional on the grammar audit; do not start
  with a model.
- **Another sequential-crew or communication variant.** Retired; no.

## Consequences

- 0.8.0 is a new world/grammar release, not another task-layer variant.
- The spatial/OOD/relational gaps get a direct test.
- The uncertain-repair line remains open for a later release.
- There is a real risk of content explosion; keep the grammar small and
  one-flagship.
