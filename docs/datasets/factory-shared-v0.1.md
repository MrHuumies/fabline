## Purpose

One shared recording of passive factory episodes, projected into several
offline tasks:

- throughput regression from vector, grid, and entity views;
- passive next-state prediction;
- masked-grid reconstruction;
- counterfactual bottleneck classification;
- (the control and control-dynamics tasks use the control environment
  separately).

The dataset is small and controlled. It is for method comparison and pipeline
validation, not for a general claim about factory data.

## Generation

- 46 episodes, 852 samples.
- World: 16×8, 500-tick limit per world, 300-tick shared episode.
- Warmup: 100–120 ticks (deterministic per-seed jitter).
- Sample interval: 10 ticks.
- Throughput horizon: 50 ticks.
- Bottleneck horizon: 30 ticks.
- Replays: 46/46 validated at suite time.

## Known biases and confounds

- **Scenario composition:** the held-out test combination is `extra_gear`;
  this is a compositional/shift proxy, not a formal causal split.
- **Small test/shift sets:** 6 episodes each. Confidence intervals are wide.
- **Predictable line:** the starter line is short and regular; the vector view
  is easier than the grid or flattened entity views.
- **Counterfactual labels:** bottleneck labels depend on the removal policy and
  the 30-tick horizon.
- **No faults, power, fluids, or multi-agent behavior.** The world is the M1/M2
  factory MVP.
