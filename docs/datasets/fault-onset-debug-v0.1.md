# Dataset card — fault-onset-debug-v0.1

- **Status:** debug dataset for the opt-in fault mechanic
- **License:** CC-BY-4.0 (synthetic data); code Apache-2.0
- **World version:** `factory-0.1.0`
- **Sensor model:** `sensors` / `diagnostics` 0.2.0 (dynamic channels)
- **Fault config:** enabled; wear per craft 1; threshold 20; repair 30 ticks
- **Generation command:** `PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python -m experiments.event_acoustic.data --out experiments/event_acoustic/data/event_views.npz --episodes 40`
- **Episodes:** 40
- **Samples:** 15,640 at 1-tick sampling
- **Positive rate:** 0.0767 (`fault_recent`); 0.0767 (`future_fault`)
- **Task:** `factory.fault.onset.v1`

## Purpose

An event-aligned detection dataset: it samples every tick and labels whether a
fault started in the last 10 ticks. It exists to test the acoustic channel at
its own timescale, after the 10-tick sampling in experiment 0011 missed the
onset spike.

It is not part of the frozen `factory-v0.1` benchmark.

## Contents

- views: `context` (7), `thermal` (2), `vibration` (2), `acoustic` (2);
- derived view for experiment 0013: `top4` (32), the top four entities by
  acoustic with mask/acoustic/vibration/temperature/wear/faulted/crafting/type;
- derived views for experiment 0014: `acoustic_agg` (2), `order4` (4),
  `order4_type` (8), `top4_true` (32), `top4_noisy` (32);
- labels: `fault_recent` (last 10 ticks), `future_fault` (next 10 ticks);
- per-sample split and episode id;
- warmup 100 ticks; samples from tick 100 to 490; 500-tick episodes;
- splits by seed: 70% train / 20% val / 10% test;
- 10,948 / 3,128 / 1,564 samples across train / val / test;
- 120 positive `fault_recent` test rows in 4 test episodes.

The dataset is generated by the experiment, not by the `fault-benchmark` CLI.
The `.npz` is ignored by git.

## Noise

Each sensor view adds independent Gaussian noise `sigma = 0.30`, clipped to
[0, 1]. The noise is dataset-level; the world remains deterministic.

## Results

Recent-onset detection, five seeds:

- neural base context + thermal: AP 0.1497;
- residual acoustic: 0.1636 (+0.0139), retention 0.0000;
- base context + thermal + vibration: AP 0.1592;
- residual acoustic: 0.1650 (+0.0058), retention 0.0000;
- GBDT context + thermal: 0.1410; + acoustic: 0.1495.

Acoustic adds a small, consistent detection gain but fails the +0.02 gate.

Top-4 entity view (experiment 0013):

- base context + thermal + vibration: AP 0.1237;
- residual aggregate acoustic: 0.1262 (+0.0025);
- residual top-4: 0.7313 (+0.6075), retention 0.0000;
- full top-4: 0.8504 (+0.7267), retention −0.0641;
- acoustic-only top-4 ablation: 0.5184 (+0.3946).

The top-4 view recovers a large onset signal. Full fine-tuning fits it better
but forgets old-view AP; the residual preserves behavior exactly.

Pooling control (experiment 0014):

- base: AP 0.1423;
- aggregate noisy mean/max: 0.2957 (+0.1534);
- order4: 0.3151 (+0.1729);
- top4_true: 0.7169 (+0.5746);
- top4_noisy: 0.7056 (+0.5633), exact retention;
- top4_noisy full: 0.7341 (+0.5919), retention −0.0234;
- post-hoc order2: 0.1616 (+0.0194), below mean/max at equal capacity;
- noisy acoustic-only ablation: 0.3305 (+0.1884), versus +0.3946 with true
  selection.

Noisy selection is not the main explanation for the full top-4 gain, but pure
rank pooling is not supported, and acoustic-only is selection-sensitive.

## Caveats

- 1-tick samples are temporally correlated; effective sample size is lower than
  the row count.
- Only 4 test episodes and 120 positive test rows; AP intervals are wide.
- Aggregate mean/max views dilute per-entity onset spikes.
- One fixed gear line, threshold, and repair time; deterministic faults.
- The 10-tick window and 1-tick sampling were fixed before the run; do not tune
  them against the result.
- The top-4 view uses pre-noise selection and 32 features; some of its gain may
  be selection or capacity rather than pooling.
- The control shows noisy selection costs only 0.0113 AP on the full view, but
  equal-capacity rank pooling is worse than mean/max and acoustic-only is
  selection-sensitive.
- Not part of `factory-v0.1`; do not compare its numbers to the frozen suite.
